Privacy Policy for Kalviro

Effective Date: August 15, 2026

1. Overview

Kalviro ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application and service handle your information.

2. Data Collection and Local Storage

Kalviro is designed with a privacy-first approach:

  • Local Data Persistence: All your daily food logs, calorie goals, custom entries, and app settings are stored locally on your device using local storage technologies. We do not transmit or store your food logs on external servers managed by us.
  • Gemini API Key: Your Google Gemini API key is stored securely on your local device and is only used to authenticate your requests directly with Google Generative AI services.

3. AI Dish Scanner & Camera Data

When you use the AI Dish Scanner feature:

  • Photos taken or selected by you are compressed on your device and sent directly to Google Generative Language APIs (Gemini) solely for nutritional analysis (detecting dish names, ingredients, weights, and estimated KBJU values).
  • We do not upload or store your photos on any proprietary intermediate servers.
  • For details on how Google handles API data, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Artificial Intelligence Disclaimer

Nutritional calculations, calorie estimations, and ingredient breakdowns provided by the AI Dish Scanner are estimates generated by artificial intelligence. They are intended for general informational and tracking purposes only and should not be used as medical or clinical dietary advice.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact us at: support@kalviro.app
